## EXECUTIVE PREFACE: HOW DEALS ARE WON BEFORE PROPOSALS ARE SENT
In competitive enterprise sales, if you are responding to an RFP (Request for Proposal) that you did not help write, **you have already lost**. The vendor that helped write the RFP has strategically set "trap-door" decision criteria that highlight their strengths and expose your fatal flaws.

Furthermore, once a customer verbally selects you, you enter the deadliest phase of enterprise sales: **The Paper Process**. More deals slip or die during legal, InfoSec, and procurement reviews than during product evaluation.

This volume provides the blueprint for ghost-writing the customer's **Decision Criteria** and orchestrating the **Paper Process** to close on schedule.

## CHAPTER 1: THE THREE TIERS OF DECISION CRITERIA

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE 3 CRITERIA TIERS                            │
├──────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. TECHNICAL CRITERIA            │ 2. BUSINESS CRITERIA                │
│ • SSO/SAML, SCIM provisioning   │ • Payback period, EBITDA impact     │
│ • Mobile native apps, CAD ingest │ • Implementation time (<3 weeks)    │
├──────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. TRAP-SETTING (SNOWFLAKE) CRITERIA                                   │
│ • Proprietary capabilities unique to [Your Product] that competitors lack   │
│ • Dynamic, real-time spatial CAD re-rendering without third-party fees│
│ • Integrated sensor-less occupancy algorithms                          │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### How to Set a Trap-Door Criterion:
During discovery, identify a unique capability of [Your Product] that competitors cannot easily match, and tie it to severe financial risk:
> *"Sarah, when you evaluate vendors, make sure you require that floor plan changes can be re-rendered in real-time by your facilities team directly. Some legacy platforms charge $1,500 per floor plan change and require a 3-week CAD outsourcing cycle. If you restructure seating quarterly across 5 floors, that’s $30,000 in hidden fees and a month of delay every single time."*

## CHAPTER 2: CONTROLLING THE PAPER PROCESS
The Paper Process encompasses every administrative step required to turn a verbal agreement into cash in the bank:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE PAPER PROCESS PIPELINE                      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Mutual NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement)                               │
│ 2. Vendor Security & InfoSec Questionnaire (SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001)   │
│ 3. Data Processing Agreement (DPA & GDPR compliance)                   │
│ 4. Master Services Agreement (MSA) Legal Redlines                      │
│ 5. Commercial Order Form & Statement of Work (SOW)                     │
│ 6. Procurement & Vendor Onboarding (Coupa, Ariba, Zip)                 │
│ 7. Authorized Signature Authority (DocuSign execution)                 │
│ 8. Purchase Order (PO) Issuance by Accounts Payable                    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The 4 Crucial Paper Process Questions to Ask in Week 2:
1. *"Who has legal signature authority for software commitments above $30,000?"*
2. *"Will your corporate counsel require us to use your standard vendor agreement, or can we redline on our standard SaaS terms?"*
3. *"Does Accounts Payable require an issued Purchase Order (PO) before cutting payment?"*
4. *"What is the standard procurement onboarding timeline in Coupa/Ariba for new technology vendors?"*
