## EXECUTIVE PREFACE: HOW TOP REPS DE-RISK PURCHASES
When a customer is paralyzed by FOMU (Fear Of Messing Up), traditional sales techniques (discounting, urging them to act, feature-dumping) cause deals to slip.

High-performing enterprise Account Executives deploy the **JOLT Protocol**, an evidence-based four-part behavioral playbook designed specifically to neutralize customer indecision and escort the buyer safely across the finish line.

## CHAPTER 2: GONG CALL TRANSCRIPT: DEFEATING INDECISION
*Context: AE [Your Name] speaking with Jennifer, VP of People at a 750-person consulting firm.*

> **Jennifer (VP People):** "[Your Name], the executive team loved the presentation. But we're nervous about rolling this out across all four offices simultaneously. If employees find desk booking frustrating, it's going to blow up on our leadership team. We think we should wait until next year to be sure."
>
> **[Your Name] (AE):** *(Executing JOLT: Judge & Offer a Recommendation)*
> "Jennifer, that hesitation is completely natural. When companies with 750 professionals transition to hybrid seating, trying to change four regional offices on Day 1 creates unnecessary operational stress. You shouldn't do a company-wide rollout on Day 1.
> Here is what we recommend: we structure this as a **Phased Rollout Agreement**.
> In Phase 1, we deploy [Your Product] exclusively to your Atlanta headquarters for 45 days. Our implementation team manages the CAD setup and white-glove onboarding for your Atlanta pilot group.
> If Atlanta hits our 90% employee booking adoption milestone, we automatically trigger Phase 2 for Chicago, Denver, and Dallas. If Atlanta doesn't hit that milestone, you have the contractual right to terminate before rolling it out nationally.
> That protects your leadership team from any organizational risk while capturing the $250k lease savings in Atlanta immediately. Jennifer, does that take the weight off your shoulders?"
>
> **Jennifer (VP People):** "That is a massive relief. If we can isolate the risk to Atlanta first, I can get that approved by the CFO tomorrow."
