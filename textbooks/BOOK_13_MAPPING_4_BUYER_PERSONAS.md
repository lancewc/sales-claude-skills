## EXECUTIVE PREFACE: THE SINGLE-THREADED GRAVEYARD
Gartner’s research reveals that the average enterprise technology purchase involves **6.8 to 10 stakeholders**. Each stakeholder belongs to a different department, operates under a distinct incentive structure, and carries veto authority over the project.

When an Account Executive is "single-threaded"—relying solely on one contact—their deal is sitting in the graveyard. If that contact leaves the company, gets overwhelmed with other projects, or fails to convince the CFO, the opportunity vanishes overnight.

Elite AEs operate as organizational cartographers: they systematically map and engage all **4 Enterprise Personas** in Week 1 of the sales cycle.

## CHAPTER 2: DEFEATING THE SHADOW BLOCKER (INFOSEC & LEGAL)
The biggest mistake in enterprise sales is waiting until Stage 4 to engage Information Security and Legal. 

When an AE announces: *"We agreed on price! Now we just need to send it to legal,"* they are walking into a buzzsaw. InfoSec reviews can take 6 weeks, and corporate legal redlines can derail a contract for 30 days unless pre-empted in Stage 2.

### The Week-2 Pre-Emption Script:
> *"Sarah, most enterprise rollouts get delayed 4 to 6 weeks in legal and IT security reviews. While you and I build the commercial business case, let's connect our security architects with your CISO team this Thursday. We will submit our SOC2 Type II, SAML SSO documentation, and standard DPA so your security gates are 100% cleared by the time the CFO approves the budget."*
