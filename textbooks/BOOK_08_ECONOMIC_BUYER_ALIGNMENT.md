## EXECUTIVE PREFACE: THE DEFINITION OF AN ECONOMIC BUYER
In John McMahon’s MEDDPICC methodology, the **Economic Buyer (EB)** is the only person in the customer's organization who possesses three distinct powers:
1. **Discretionary Checkbook Authority:** They can release funds that were not formally budgeted.
2. **Veto Power:** They can unilaterally kill a project even if every department manager loves it.
3. **Reallocation Authority:** They can pull $50,000 from marketing or HR and give it to facilities to fund your software.

If you have never spoken with the Economic Buyer, **your deal is unforecastable**. You are relying on second-hand hearsay from subordinates who rarely understand how executive capital allocation decisions are made.

## CHAPTER 2: HOW TO ACCESS THE EB WITHOUT ALIENATING YOUR CHAMPION
The number one fear of a sales rep is offending their champion: *"If I ask to meet their boss, they will get angry and think I don't trust them."*

Elite AEs never bypass the champion; they **elevate** the champion.

### The 4-Step Access Framework:
1. **Praise their domain expertise:** Acknowledge that the champion knows the operational details better than anyone.
2. **Warn them of executive hazards:** Explain that CFOs grill project sponsors on complex financial metrics (ASC 842 lease capitalization, EBITDA arbitrage) that software quotes don't cover.
3. **Offer joint sponsorship:** Position the meeting as a co-presentation where the champion shines as the strategic innovator.
4. **Draft the invitation:** Write the exact email your champion can forward to the EB.

## CHAPTER 3: THE 20-MINUTE EXECUTIVE BRIEFING BLUEPRINT
When you finally earn 20 minutes with the CFO or COO, **never give a software demo**. Follow this strict executive agenda:

- **Minute 0–3 (Strategic Alignment):** Verify their macro corporate mandate (e.g., *"Dan, Laura mentioned your executive priority this fiscal year is cutting real estate OpEx by 15% while supporting hybrid flexibility. Is that accurate?"*)
- **Minute 4–10 (The Quantified Problem):** Show their current financial bleed using their actual real estate lease metrics.
- **Minute 11–15 (The Business Case & EBITDA Impact):** Present the 1-page financial model showing net EBITDA expansion and payback in under 60 days.
- **Minute 16–20 (Decision Process & Next Steps):** Agree on the commercial criteria required to greenlight the contract: *"Dan, if our 30-day technical validation proves this $450k lease reduction, do you see any barrier to approving this agreement by October 31st?"*
