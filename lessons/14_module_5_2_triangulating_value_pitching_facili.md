## 1. Core Operating Directive & Theory

### Speaking in Four Dialects

        
A rookie AE pitches the exact same narrative to every stakeholder. An enterprise AE changes dialects depending on who is sitting across the table.

        

          

            <strong class="text-brand-400 block mb-0.5">When Talking to Facilities & Workplace:</strong>
            Focus on eliminating manual Visio edits, dynamic moves, self-service seating, and automated desk sanitation.
          

          

            <strong class="text-emerald-400 block mb-0.5">When Talking to Corporate Real Estate & CFO:</strong>
            Focus on square foot utilization analytics, lease restructuring, CapEx avoidance, and 10x ROI models.
          

          

            <strong class="text-amber-400 block mb-0.5">When Talking to HR & People Leadership:</strong>
            Focus on employee retention, seamless collaborative neighborhood seating, and eliminating RTO return-to-office friction.
          

          

            <strong class="text-indigo-400 block mb-0.5">When Talking to IT & Security:</strong>
            Focus on Okta/Azure AD SSO, automated SCIM provisioning, SOC2 Type II compliance, and zero on-prem server footprint.

**Question:** When presenting to a Chief Information Officer (CIO) or VP of IT, which topic should you prioritize?

* [A] The decorative aesthetic of interactive floor plan icons.
* [B] Single Sign-On (SSO), automated employee SCIM provisioning, and SOC2 InfoSec compliance. ✓ [CORRECT]
* [C] Discounts on physical conference room chairs.
* [D] The history of your marketing department.

> **Strategic Rationale:** IT leaders care primarily about security, identity management, compliance, and minimizing ongoing IT support overhead.
