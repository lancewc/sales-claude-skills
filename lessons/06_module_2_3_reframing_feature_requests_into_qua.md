# Module 2.3: Reframing Feature Requests into Quantified Business Bleed

## 1. Core Operating Directive & Theory

### The Feature-Trap: How Amateur Reps Lose Control

        
Prospects are conditioned to treat software reps like drive-thru order takers: *"Do you have QR code desk check-in? Do you integrate with Slack? Can I color-code my desks?"*

        
If you simply answer *"Yes, yes, and yes,"* you have commoditized yourself. You are now competing on a feature checklist where a cheaper competitor can claim they do the exact same thing.

        

          #### The 'Acknowledge & Pivot' Reframing Formula

          
When asked for a feature, use this 3-step pivot:

          

            * **Validate & Confirm:** *"Yes, we support native QR check-in and automated desk release..."*
            * **Probe the Context:** *"...but usually when teams ask for that, it's because people are 'ghost booking' desks and not showing up. Is that what's happening on your floors?"*
            * **Quantify the Business Bleed:** *"When ghost booking happens, what percentage of your desks sit empty while other employees think the office is full?"*
          

        

        

          The AE Law:
          Never demonstrate a feature until the prospect has explained the economic cost of not having that feature.

## 2. Interactive Knowledge & Comprehension Check

**Question:** When a prospect asks 'Does [Your Product] have an integration with Microsoft Teams?', what is the elite AE response?

* [A] 'Yes we do! Let me send you a screenshot of the integration right now.'
* [B] 'Yes, absolutely. Just curious, how are your teams currently using Teams for hybrid scheduling, and what breaks when they try to coordinate in-office days today?' ✓ [CORRECT]
* [C] 'No, nobody really uses Teams anymore, you should switch to Slack.'
* [D] 'Only if you buy our Enterprise Tier package.'

> **Strategic Rationale:** Confirming the capability while immediately probing their current operational friction keeps you in diagnostic control and exposes the underlying problem.
