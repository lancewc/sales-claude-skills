## 1. Core Operating Directive & Theory

### The 4 Behaviors of the JOLT Playbook

        
Top 1% AEs execute the **JOLT** behaviors to guide indecisive buyers across the finish line:

        

          

            <strong class="text-brand-300 block mb-1">J – Judge the Indecision:</strong>
            Diagnose the root: Is it *Information Overload* (asking for more demos), *Valuation Doubt* (uncertainty about ROI), or *Execution Risk* (fear of user adoption)?
          

          

            <strong class="text-indigo-300 block mb-1">O – Offer a Recommendation:</strong>
            Stop offering open menus. Act like a trusted physician: *"Based on 50 hybrid rollouts identical to your firm, here is the exact tier and rollout schedule you should take."*
          

          

            <strong class="text-amber-300 block mb-1">L – Limit the Exploration:</strong>
            Shut down endless rabbit holes: *"We've verified 95% of your requirements; let's test that obscure edge case in onboarding rather than delaying your lease go-live."*
          

          

            <strong class="text-emerald-300 block mb-1">T – Take Risk Off the Table:</strong>
            Provide psychological safety nets: phased rollouts, dedicated customer success architects, and milestone sign-offs.

## 2. Interactive Knowledge & Comprehension Check

**Question:** What is the recommended JOLT behavior when a prospect suffers from choice overload and requests 5 pricing tier permutations?

* [A] Send all 5 proposals immediately and tell them to pick one.
* [B] Offer a Recommendation: Stop presenting open-ended menus and prescribe the exact tier optimal for their specific situation based on proven peer benchmarks. ✓ [CORRECT]
* [C] Refuse to send any pricing.
* [D] Double the price to create artificial prestige.

> **Strategic Rationale:** Indecisive buyers crave proactive guidance. Offering a definitive recommendation eliminates choice paralysis and builds trusted-advisor status.
